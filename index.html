Ultra Unlock: 10th Anniversary — IV Keepers

PvP ranks from pvpoke (pulled 2026-07-21). green = capped PvP keep low ATK / high DEF+HP. orange = Master/Raid/Mega keep high ATK (IV% ≥96 / 15 ATK). grey = collection keep 1 best/shiny.

Capped-PvP keeper bar — read Poke Genie's Rank % (the percentile, e.g. “Rank 85.64%”) — not the appraisal star %:
  • 🏆 Tournament / perfect — Rank % ≥ 97.6  (≤ #100)
  • ✅ Keep & main it (recommended) — Rank % ≥ 95  (≤ #205)
  • 👌 Fine if it’s your only copy — Rank % ≥ 88  (≤ #500)
…only for a top-~50 species that also reaches the CP cap. Master / Raid / Mega ignore this — use appraisal IV% ≥ 96 / 15 ATK.
