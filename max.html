How to read this

  • Only Max-Battle-caught Pokémon can be used (a normal wild/raid catch of the same species can't Dynamax).
  • G-Max = unique form + stronger exclusive move (tops the attacker tables); D-Max = generic typed Max Moves.
  • Roles: Max Attack (damage) / Max Guard (team shield) / Max Spirit (heal). Bring 1–2 attackers + a bulky Guard/Spirit anchor.
  • IV by role: attackers → high ATK; anchors → high HP + DEF. Max Attack type follows your fast move (pick STAB).
