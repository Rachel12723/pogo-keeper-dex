How Max Battles work (the essentials)

  • You can only use Max-Battle-caught Pokémon. A normal wild/egg/raid Pokémon cannot Dynamax and cannot enter Max Battles — even if it's the same species. Only ones caught from Power Spot / Max Battles are Max-capable (they share candy + dex entry with normal ones, nothing else).
  • Dynamax vs Gigantamax. Most Max species just Dynamax (grow big, generic typed Max Moves). A special subset can Gigantamax — unique look + a stronger exclusive G-Max Move (higher power), caught from harder G-Max battles. A G-Max individual can also Dynamax; a plain Dynamax one cannot Gigantamax.
  • Three Max-move roles (pick one each Dynamax turn): Max Attack deal damage (typed; G-Max signature hits hardest) · Max Guard team shield (tank) · Max Spirit heal the team (healer).
  • Bring a balanced trio, not 3 glass cannons. Typical team = 1–2 attackers (high ATK, ideally G-Max) + a bulky Guard/Spirit Pokémon to shield and heal through the boss's Max phase. Level, best moves, and the right type still matter.

Dynamax (non-G-Max) — large & rotating

Dozens of species cycle through Max Battles (~44 across tiers at any time). Notable Dynamax picks seen in 2026 include the legendary beasts (Raikou / Entei / Suicune), Kubfu → Urshifu, Zacian / Zamazenta, Eternatus, the Kanto/Galar starters, and many bulky mons that make good Max Guard / Max Spirit anchors.
